# Lancee Workspace

## Investor Overview

**One workspace to win work, deliver it, get paid, and make better business decisions.**

Lancee is a business operating workspace for freelancers, studios, agencies, and growing service businesses. It brings the entire commercial cycle into one place: leads become clients, client requests become projects, projects move through delivery and approval, completed work becomes an invoice, and invoices become verified payments.

The investment case is straightforward. Small service businesses lose margin every day to fragmented tools, duplicated administration, missed follow-ups, slow approvals, and late payments. Lancee converts that operational leakage into productive capacity and cash flow. It gives owners the control of a larger operation without forcing them to build a larger back office.

Lancee is already a complete working environment, not a collection of disconnected feature ideas. It combines client management, project delivery, communications, files, invoicing, payments, automation, analytics, collaboration, AI assistance, and decision intelligence in a single product designed around how service businesses make money.

---

## The Commercial Opportunity

Independent professionals and small teams are often running meaningful businesses through a patchwork of email, spreadsheets, messaging apps, cloud folders, task boards, accounting tools, and manual reminders. Each additional system creates another subscription, another handoff, and another place for revenue-critical work to disappear.

Lancee replaces that fragmentation with one connected operating layer. The commercial value comes from five levers:

1. **More billable capacity** — routine administration and repeatable workflows are automated, allowing the same team to serve more clients.
2. **Faster revenue conversion** — projects move cleanly from request to delivery, approval, invoice, and payment.
3. **Lower software and coordination cost** — core operations are consolidated instead of spread across multiple specialist tools.
4. **Higher client retention** — communication, deadlines, files, feedback, and delivery history stay organised and visible.
5. **Better management decisions** — owners can see live operational and financial signals and compare decisions with measured outcomes.

For investors, this positions Lancee as recurring operational infrastructure. Once a business manages its clients, delivery history, files, workflows, invoices, and team activity inside Lancee, the product becomes embedded in daily operations and increasingly valuable over time.

## Revenue-Creating and Cost-Saving Capabilities

### A live command centre for the owner

The Home workspace gives owners an immediate view of active projects, approaching deadlines, outstanding invoices, useful automations, and recent activity. Date, local time, location, and weather provide practical daily context without turning the page into a technical dashboard.

**Business value:** less time assembling status updates, earlier intervention on late work, and daily visibility of cash still to be collected.

### A client record that protects revenue relationships

Lancee maintains a searchable client directory with contact details, status, branding, project counts, linked work, and matching mailbox history. Each client has a focused workspace, giving the business a durable relationship record instead of scattering context across individual inboxes.

**Business value:** faster account servicing, smoother handovers, more consistent follow-up, and reduced risk when a team member is unavailable or leaves.

### Project delivery built around margin

Every project is searchable and filterable, with ownership, deadlines, status, progress, deliverables, attachments, external links, and connected Drive resources. Inside each project, work moves through a persistent Kanban board with task checklists and notes. Custom work buckets allow teams to reflect their actual delivery process.

Creating a project also prepares the commercial path by creating its job card and draft invoice. This makes billing part of delivery from the beginning instead of an administrative afterthought.

**Business value:** fewer missed steps, better capacity control, reduced project overruns, faster onboarding, and less revenue lost because completed work was never billed promptly.

### Client reviews that reduce rework

Lancee separates internal delivery from client feedback. Teams create durable review packages with deadlines, item-level approval states, comments, image previews, and a permanent audit history. Secure, expiring review links give clients controlled access without exposing the wider workspace.

Visual work can be reviewed directly through rectangle and polygon annotations. Feedback includes comments, category, and priority, while the delivery team can move each annotation through open, in-progress, resolved, or rejected states. Client approval advances the draft invoice toward collection.

**Business value:** shorter approval cycles, fewer ambiguous revision requests, defensible scope history, and lower non-billable rework.

### Invoicing and payments that accelerate cash flow

Lancee creates professional A4 invoices in four brand-colour styles. Businesses can add their own bank-transfer details or issue hosted Paystack payment links. Successful payments are verified and reconciled without creating duplicate records.

The Invoicing workspace keeps billing close to the client and project records that produced it. Outstanding amounts and pending invoices flow into Home and Analytics, keeping receivables visible to the owner.

**Business value:** invoices go out sooner, clients have a simpler path to pay, finance status stays visible, and manual reconciliation effort falls.

### Business email that can trigger work

The Messages workspace connects the business mailbox, organises folders, searches server-side, reads messages safely, and supports compose and reply. Common providers are discovered automatically, while guided setup covers other professional mail services.

Incoming messages can trigger automations based on sender, recipient, subject, or keywords. A client email can create a project, job card, and draft invoice as one repeatable action, with duplicate protection.

**Business value:** faster response to demand, less copying from email into project systems, fewer forgotten requests, and a shorter path from enquiry to billable work.

### Files and cloud work without the scavenger hunt

The Files workspace provides a searchable document library with folders, previews, downloads, uploads, moves, and practical action menus. Local workspace storage is always available. Google Drive can become the workspace cloud root, with nested navigation, folder creation, file viewing, document editing, uploads, and saves back to Drive. Dropbox and OneDrive destinations are also supported when enabled.

Lancee opens images and PDFs inside the workspace and supports working document experiences for supported cloud files. Reports, presentations, executive briefs, and PDFs created by the assistant become normal downloadable workspace files rather than disappearing inside a chat response.

**Business value:** less time locating the latest version, fewer delivery mistakes, easier client servicing, and greater return from cloud storage businesses already pay for.

### Ideas that stay connected to execution

The Ideas workspace provides full visual whiteboards with freehand drawing, shapes, arrows, text, images, embedded content, reusable libraries, export, and named boards. Previously opened canvases remain editable offline.

**Business value:** discovery, workshop thinking, campaign concepts, process maps, and early project thinking remain attached to the operating workspace, reducing the gap between an idea and paid execution.

### Automation that gives small teams operating leverage

Lancee turns plain-language routines into active, repeatable automations. Built-in workflow recipes cover workspace health, project reviews, client directory reviews, invoice reviews, project status changes, and draft invoice creation. Automations can run on demand or on schedules, and mailbox events can start relevant workflows.

Every run is durable and visible. Results open as readable outcome cards, while detailed outputs and execution history remain available for accountability. More advanced businesses can connect n8n for signed, auditable workflows across external systems.

**Business value:** administrative work is completed consistently, experienced staff spend less time on repeatable coordination, and growing firms add transaction volume without matching headcount growth one-for-one.

### Connections that preserve existing technology investments

Lancee is designed to become the operating layer, not another silo. Its searchable connection catalogue exposes a broad provider ecosystem with official provider information and owner-controlled authorisation. Google Drive, Paystack, n8n, business email, WhatsApp owner notifications, and Lancee’s own business tools are managed from one Connections area.

Businesses can request additional systems without being misled into thinking an unsupported connection is already active. Connected credentials remain protected, while sensitive actions stay under owner control.

**Business value:** lower switching friction, faster adoption, and the ability to improve operations without abandoning tools that already work.

### AI assistance that produces business output

The Lancee workspace assistant operates with the context of the current business. It can answer questions about the workspace, research public information, prepare documents, create reports and presentation-ready artifacts, work with files, and propose operational actions. Higher-impact actions pause for explicit approval.

Research-to-report work follows a controlled flow: sourced research is reviewed first, then the finished report is created and stored in Files. Persistent task threads retain plans, progress, results, and approvals even when work is interrupted.

For advanced users, the embedded Codex Workspace brings a capable work agent into a controlled workspace, while approved external agent devices can receive narrowly scoped access.

**Business value:** owners turn questions into usable deliverables faster, reduce outsourced research and document-production effort, and gain an always-available operational assistant without giving up control.

### Decision Intelligence: from instinct to institutional memory

Lancee records important business decisions, what management expected to happen, the evidence available at the time, and the outcome that followed. Reviews can be scheduled for the right future date, and the workspace notifies the team when measurement is due.

When a new decision resembles a historical one, Lancee compares the structure, context, evidence quality, and recency of the earlier decision. It keeps measured facts separate from interpretation and never turns correlation into a causal claim. Management can confirm, correct, or reject the contextual assessment without erasing the original machine record.

This gives even a small firm something normally associated with a sophisticated organisation: a durable, reviewable record of why choices were made and what happened next.

**Business value:** fewer repeated mistakes, better pricing and delivery choices, faster management reviews, preserved organisational knowledge, and more disciplined allocation of time and capital.

### Analytics that connect activity to management action

Live Analytics shows open projects, work due this week, outstanding invoice value, client count, active automations, connected tools, workflow volume, execution success rate, runtime, run duration, pending invoices, and upcoming project risk. Data can be refreshed and exported.

**Business value:** owners can manage exceptions instead of chasing updates, quantify automation adoption, focus collections, and see whether operating volume is scaling cleanly.

### Team collaboration without losing control

Owners can invite team members through expiring links, assign owner, collaborator, or viewer roles, filter the team directory, edit access, and disable members. Workspace identity and role boundaries apply across client data, files, automations, connected systems, and AI actions.

Notifications keep the team aware of mail, automation outcomes, decision reviews, and related work, with direct navigation and read controls.

**Business value:** faster onboarding, clearer accountability, reduced key-person risk, and controlled delegation as the business grows.

### Fast, tailored onboarding

The Workspace Builder guides a new owner through a ten-step business setup in plain language. It recommends the smallest useful module set, prepares approved integrations and automation drafts, applies identity and timezone, and can add clearly labelled sample data. Owners can return to the builder as the business changes.

**Business value:** shorter time to value, less implementation support, better feature adoption, and a workspace shaped around the customer’s operating model.

### Anywhere access and continuity

Lancee is installable as a progressive web app. Its core application experience remains available as an installed workspace, while previously opened Ideas boards can be edited offline. Responsive layouts keep long project, file, and communication views usable across desktop and mobile.

**Business value:** the operating workspace remains close to the owner and team, whether they are in the office, with a client, or working in the field.

### Trust, access, and business continuity

Lancee gives each business a private workspace with secure sign-in, email-confirmed registration, protected sessions, invitation-based team access, owner-controlled permissions, and clear approval steps for sensitive actions. Workspace settings centralise business identity, authentication, notifications, profile imagery, and account controls. Connected service credentials stay protected and are never exposed in everyday screens.

The workspace retains durable project, automation, agent, approval, and activity history, so an interrupted session does not mean lost work. Direct links return users to the right workspace page, and notifications lead people back to the client, workflow, message, or review that needs attention.

**Business value:** stronger customer trust, lower operational risk, reliable accountability, and less revenue disruption from lost context or uncontrolled access.

### Commercial packaging that expands with the customer

Lancee includes a public product experience, self-service registration, email confirmation, guided sign-in, clear plan selection, region-aware pricing, trial visibility, and account billing status. Solo, Pro, and Studio packages expand automation and team capacity as the customer’s operation grows, while add-on categories create room for additional automation, forms, payments, storage, and seats.

The public experience explains the complete business journey, connected tools, security posture, workflows, and Decision Intelligence in outcome-led language, giving the platform a built-in path from discovery to activated workspace.

**Business value:** lower acquisition and onboarding friction, a natural land-and-expand motion, and monetisation aligned with customer growth and product usage.

### Platform control at scale

The administration workspace provides global user and workspace directories, signup control, API traffic and error analytics, automation and agent logs, performance timing, runtime metrics, and database health. Public registration can be opened or paused without affecting existing customers or invited users.

**Business value:** lower support and operating cost per customer, faster issue resolution, stronger service oversight, and controlled growth of the customer base.

## The Financial Logic

Lancee’s return can be measured without relying on speculative claims. For each customer, the core monthly value equation is:

> **Value created = administrative hours recovered + avoidable software spend removed + contribution margin from added capacity + cash-flow benefit from faster collection + rework and leakage avoided**

An investor or buyer can model the outcome using the business’s own numbers:

| Value driver | Practical calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Administration recovered | Hours saved per month × fully loaded hourly cost |
| Tool consolidation | Monthly cost of tools replaced or downgraded |
| Added delivery capacity | Additional billable hours × realised gross margin per hour |
| Faster collection | Reduction in debtor days × average daily invoiced revenue × cost of capital |
| Reduced rework | Avoided revision hours × fully loaded delivery cost |
| Reduced leakage | Previously missed or delayed billable items recovered |

The strongest economic effect is cumulative. Saving a few hours in one workflow is useful; connecting client intake, project setup, delivery, approval, invoicing, and payment compounds that saving across every customer engagement.

Its advantage is not simply having many features. It is the economic connection between them:

- An email becomes a project.
- A project begins with a draft invoice already prepared.
- A client review advances work toward billing.
- A payment updates the financial picture.
- An automation records its outcome.

That closed operating loop gives Lancee a credible route to become the system of record for the service-business economy: simple enough for an independent professional, powerful enough for a growing studio, and commercially aligned with the outcomes both care about most—more capacity, better margins, faster cash, and stronger control.
