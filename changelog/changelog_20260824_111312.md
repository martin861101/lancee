# Connected Intelligence Human-Facing Redesign — Phase 1B

Date: 2026-08-24 11:13:12 UTC

## Implemented

- Replaced the technical Connected Intelligence dashboard with a concise Lancee
  briefing driven by the Phase 1A summary states: `attention_needed`,
  `all_clear`, and `insufficient_activity`.
- Added accessible local views for **Things I’ve noticed** and **Lancee
  activity**, with findings as the default.
- Split the former monolithic page into focused briefing, tab, findings, card,
  explanation drawer, activity, inspection, avatar, icon, and presentation
  modules.
- Added deterministic human presentation for `client_attention_load` and
  `project_meeting_load`, including restrained severity, factual metrics,
  careful possible explanations, and contextual checks.
- Connected finding actions to the existing Projects and Clients navigation.
- Reworked finding evidence into progressive disclosure. Human explanation is
  first; the unchanged authoritative relationships, observed records,
  comparison set, detector condition, percentile/threshold, confidence,
  detector identifiers, and exact workspace-event references remain available
  under **View technical evidence**.
- Replaced Decision Intelligence History in the current UI with a vertical
  timeline backed by the real activity list/detail APIs. Activity detail omits
  missing and zero-value count placeholders and never exposes raw IDs.
- Centralized the six supplied Lancee assets from `public/img/lancee/` in a
  reusable avatar component, with deterministic briefing and activity states.
- Added restrained CSS motion, reduced-motion support, narrow-width layouts,
  wrapping technical evidence, keyboard tabs, disclosure state, Escape close,
  focus restoration, and visible focus treatment.

## Preserved

- No Connected Intelligence schema, detector threshold, workspace-event,
  opportunity generation/deduplication, Mail/Calendar instrumentation, MCP, or
  Hermes behavior changed.
- Legacy Decision Dynamics storage, services, APIs, and backend verification
  remain intact; only its current user-facing history section was removed.
