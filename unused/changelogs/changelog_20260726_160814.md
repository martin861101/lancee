# Changelog — 2026-07-26 16:08:14 UTC

## Freelancer and small-business product pivot

- Repositioned lancee from an agent-operations product to a portable operating
  workspace for freelancers and small business owners.
- Rebuilt the primary navigation around **Home**, **Work**, **Ideas**,
  **Automations**, **Connections**, and **Money**.
- Added a travel-aware client and project workspace with priorities, deadlines,
  project progress, clients, and a functional in-memory new-project flow.
- Added a responsive ideas canvas with briefs, visual references, colour
  palettes, tasks, notes, zoom controls, board tabs, and quick capture.
- Added an invoicing and payments workspace with revenue summaries, invoice
  states, expected payments, draft creation, and clearly labelled setup
  previews for Stripe, PayPal, and Paystack.
- Reframed agents as plain-language automations and replaced enterprise lead,
  support, and contract examples with realistic design-studio routines.
- Updated the landing page, authentication story, Home dashboard, connection
  catalog, notification examples, settings, and sample data around a travelling
  independent liquor-label designer.
- Kept AI as a secondary, optional helper rather than the primary product or
  navigation model.
- Added `PRODUCT_VISION.md` with the audience, jobs to be done, product
  principles, information architecture, integration roles, reference workflow,
  honest data states, success metrics, and phased roadmap.
- Updated README, getting-started, and integration documentation to match the
  new product direction and placeholder boundaries.
