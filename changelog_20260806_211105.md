# WhatsApp connection recovery

- Added bounded automatic reconnect with exponential backoff for transient
  Baileys socket closures such as `428 Connection Closed`.
- Restored saved WhatsApp sessions during backend startup when auth files are
  available and preserved manual logout and self-number mismatch safeguards.
- Added a short retry window for platform notifications during reconnects and
  explicit skipped-delivery logging.
- Added a visible WhatsApp connection-state badge with Connected, Connecting,
  Waiting for QR scan, Connection error, and Not connected states.
- Extended `pnpm verify:whatsapp` to cover reconnect and session restoration.
